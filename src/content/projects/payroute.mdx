Payroute is an x402 payment rail on Mantle. I was the sole author of the escrow
contracts and shipped a TypeScript SDK (v1.0.1) so other teams could integrate
without writing raw contract calls.

## The problem

Micropayments need a way to hold funds until a service is delivered, then release
them without a trusted intermediary. The rail also has to be easy for client
applications to adopt — a correct contract is not enough if integration takes a
week.

## What I built

- Escrow contracts with explicit states for funded, delivered, released, and
  refunded payments.
- Foundry tests covering the happy path plus replay, double-release, and
  unauthorised-release cases.
- A TypeScript SDK that wraps contract reads, writes, and event decoding behind a
  small typed API, published as v1.0.1.

## What I learned

The SDK was the product. Once integration was reduced to a few typed calls,
teams could evaluate the rail in an afternoon instead of reading the contracts
line by line.
