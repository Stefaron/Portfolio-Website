## Abstract

Batik Keraton motifs carry distinct visual patterns that are difficult to
separate with hand-crafted features, especially when the dataset is small and
class balance is uneven. This study evaluates transfer learning as a practical
approach for classifying four Keraton motif classes.

## Method

A ResNet-50 backbone pre-trained on ImageNet was fine-tuned on a curated dataset
of Keraton batik images. The training pipeline used augmentation and gradual
unfreezing to adapt the deeper layers without overfitting the limited samples.
Performance was measured on a held-out test split alongside validation accuracy.

## Results

The fine-tuned model reached **97.8% test accuracy** and **96.8% validation
accuracy** across the four motif classes. The gap between the two suggests the
model generalised rather than memorising the training set, though the small
number of classes remains a limitation.

## Takeaways

Fine-tuning a strong vision backbone is a realistic baseline for cultural
heritage image classification. The paper documents the preprocessing and
training choices so the result can be reproduced on a larger motif set.
